About Me

Hello, I am Royanna Iman and I was born and raised on O'ahu. My first exposure to learning more about graphic designing and filmmaking was in middle school when I took a class that incorporated learning Adobe products like Adobe After Effects, Premiere Pro, and Illustrator. I didn't return to the idea of media until my junior and senior years of high school, when I took graphic arts classes as my electives and learned more about Adobe Illustrator. I then dove straight into it when I decided to major in Creative Media at the University of Hawai'i at West O'ahu. Since then, I have learned so much about the fundamentals of filmmaking and designing and I am eager to practice them in a professional setting.
